About

I am a research engineer at Huawei in Zürich, Switzerland currently working at the intersection of computer graphics and generative models. I am interested in generation and reconstruction problems through the lens of rendering.

Before joining Huawei I worked on production renderers for animated movies: at Animal Logic (then Netflix Animation Studios) where I was part of the Rendering R&D team and at Tangent Animation on a custom fork of Blender's Cycles. You can find me in the credits of Leo, The Magician's Elephant and Maya and the Three.

I hold a MSc from the University of British Columbia, where I worked on image vectorization algorithms under the supervision of dr. Alla Sheffer. My BSc thesis was on WebGL image retargeting under the supervision of dr. Marco Tarini.

Control-DINO: Feature Space Conditioning for Controllable Image-to-Video Diffusion

Edoardo A. Dominici*, Thomas Deixelberger*, Konstantinos Vardis, Markus Steinberger

ECCV 2026
TL;DR: A DINO-conditioned ControlNet for image-to-video diffusion that is appearance invariant offering semantic and structural control. We show how it can be applied to Video Transfer and Video-from-3D.

Scene Generation at Absolute Scale: Utilizing Semantic and Geometric Guidance From Text for Accurate and Interpretable 3D Indoor Scene Generation

Stefan Ainetter, Thomas Deixelberger, Edoardo A. Dominici, Philipp Drescher, Konstantinos Vardis, Markus Steinberger

ECCV 2026
TL;DR: We use a metric-scale geometric proxy to drive scene generation through multi-view diffusion models. We introduce a multimodal panorama generation model to generate starting frames from which we efficiently sample trajectories for world exploration and for 3DGS optimization.
